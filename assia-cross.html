ABOUT ASSIA
CROSS.

The Khabtoura Cross is located in a beautiful area of ​​the town of Assia and was recently built in 2024 through the efforts of the town's young men and women volunteers and local donors. The idea originated with the Youth of the Virgin Mary Brotherhood in Assia, in cooperation with Saint George Endowment Committee, which donated the land for the cross.

The project is characterized by a spirit of community cooperation, with the townspeople contributing out of their love for their faith and their town. Each person contributed a portion of their land, which helped to widen the road leading to the cross and seamlessly connect it to the surrounding areas.

The cross was celebrated for the first time on September 14, 2024, and the annual celebration continued, making the cross a focal point for religious and social gatherings in the town. Despite its recent construction, the cross symbolizes unity, cooperation, and love for the town and its faith, and it attracts visitors and worshippers thanks to its beautiful location and the efforts of the local community in building it.
