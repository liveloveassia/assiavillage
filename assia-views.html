Assia Views

Discover the breathtaking panoramic views and stunning landscapes of Assia Village, Lebanon. From mountain vistas to sunset horizons, experience the natural beauty of this charming destination.

About Assia Views

Views that can be seen from the town:
If we look towards the north, we see:

First: Jebla, Awra, Da'el, Halta...

Second: Al-Majdal Koura, the entire Koura district, Deir Bella, Ain Akrin, Miziara, Ehden, Our Lady of the Fortress (Ehden), Bsharri, the Cedars, Qornet el-Sawda, and its surroundings...

Third (Tall buildings...): Tripoli Town, Tripoli Port, Al-Mina, Zgharta district, Bnachii...

Fourth: Mount Tarbel...

Fifth (If the weather is not humid): The coast and city of Tartus (Syria), Arwad Island (Syria), the cities of Latakia and Homs (Syria)...

Sixth (If the weather is very clear): The Turkish Mount Kılıç (Turkey) can be vaguely seen.
