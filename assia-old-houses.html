OLD HOUSES.

The old houses of Assia Village stand as timeless testaments to the region's rich history and architectural heritage. Built with local stone and traditional techniques, these charming homes reflect the village's past, offering a glimpse into the daily life and craftsmanship of earlier generations. Preserving these structures helps maintain Assia's cultural identity and provides visitors with an authentic experience of Lebanese rural heritage.
