Ain Blat is a historic spring located in Assia Village, Lebanon. This serene natural landmark is surrounded by lush greenery and offers a peaceful retreat for visitors.

ABOUT AIN
BLAT.

Ain El Blat is one of the beautiful areas in Assia, and is considered one of the distinctive natural places. It is distinguished by springs of fresh water that emerge from the mountains, and constitutes a source of comfort and refreshment for visitors. The surrounding area is full of green nature, and the atmosphere is calm and suitable for picnics. Ain El Blat is also considered a favorite destination for photography and nature enthusiasts, because it offers stunning views and a peaceful atmosphere. Ain El Blat is also distinguished by the presence of some natural trails that are suitable for lovers of walking and hiking, and it will be a wonderful opportunity to explore and learn about the surrounding nature. There are also some places designated for sitting and resting, so that visitors can enjoy the tranquility and spend a pleasant time surrounded by nature. In addition, the area is a meeting point for many local visitors and tourists, and reflects part of Lebanon's natural beauty.
