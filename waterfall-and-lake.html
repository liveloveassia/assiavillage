About Assia Waterfall and Lake

The Waterfall and Lake of Our Lady of the Fortress in the town of Assia, in the Batroun district, is one of the most prominent natural and tourist destinations in the region and Lebanon, and one of the top 100 water features and sources in Lebanon. The lake is a natural cavity approximately 20 meters deep, and the area resembles the famous "Balou' Balaa" sinkhole in its shape. It is surrounded by rocks covered with moss and green vegetation, giving it a unique natural beauty.

The waterfall that flows into the lake is considered one of the natural wonders of Lebanon.

To visit this site, it is recommended to reach the town of Assia, then head towards the Church of Our Lady of the Fortress or the Mrah-al-Hajj bridge, from where you can reach the waterfall and lake on foot via mountain trails. It is advisable to wear appropriate hiking shoes, especially during the winter and spring seasons, when the weather is mild and the site is at its most beautiful.
