About Assia Snow

When winter blankets Assia Village in snow, it transforms into a magical wonderland. The pristine white landscapes, snow-covered rooftops, and frosty mountain vistas create a serene and enchanting atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy winter activities like snow walks, photography, and the rare opportunity to experience a snowy paradise in Lebanon. This seasonal beauty highlights the village's natural charm and offers a unique contrast to its usual Mediterranean allure.
