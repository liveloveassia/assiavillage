About Assia Village

Known for its stunning natural beauty and peaceful atmosphere. Assia offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountains.

the name of Assia ( ܐܣܝܐ ) meaning Doctor in Suryoyo and Aramic. It is a picturesque town in the Mountains of Batroun located on a high mountain at an altitude (with its outskirts) from 750m to approximately 1100m. It is on an area of 945 hectares, but the area of the village is in reality more than that. It is about 975 hectares, so it is among the first five ranks the largest areas in Batroun District. The town of Assia is bordered by more than 12 villages, and it is considered the largest area in the Batroun district in terms of the number of villages surrounding it. They are: Tartej, Hadtoun, Rasha, Alali, Marah Al-Hajj, Chobtine, Zan, Kfarshleiman, Helta, Jebla, Dael, Aura, Qandoula, Beshtoudar, Bchaaleh, Nahla, ...

Assia has about 25 neighborhoods and three regions (Yarita, Sherife, Brarekha).

However, the town of Assia did not use this machine, and it remained in the old traditional method without any kind of development intervening in it, and the industry remained the focus of attention. The Assia Castle history part 1
Since ancient times, there was a castle or, in other words, a palace of the kingdom at this location. Only a few stones remain of it next to Saint George church. If we walk around the church, we see the entrance to a cave (the treasury of Saint Assia), and on the other side, we see the sarcophagi where the children and servants of the kingdom were buried. We also see, on another side, but blocked with stones, the entrance to a tunnel through which the queen would escape from the castle to the "Shumays Valley" during attacks (according to history). The queen who owned the castle here also owned the castle in Beshtoudar, which is called the "Fortress Castle".

The Fortress Castle history part 2
In the area of Beshtoudar, there is a mountain called "Mount Al-Hajar" (the mountain of stone). In it, there is a castle called the "Fortress Castle" (Qal'at Al-Hisn). This castle is located on the highest peak in the region. From there, the whole region can be observed.
Inside the castle, there are 3 reservoirs. There are also very deep rooms. According to history, the queen who owned the castle in Assia also owned this castle in Beshtoudar. She used to come down from the castle here (Al-Hisn) and go to the castle in Assia through a tunnel that connected the two castles. This is according to what the elders of the region say.
