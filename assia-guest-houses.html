About Assia Guest Houses

Assia Village offers charming guest houses that blend traditional Lebanese architecture with modern comforts. Stay in historic stone houses, experience authentic village life, and enjoy warm hospitality. These accommodations provide a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of Assia, surrounded by stunning mountain views and serene landscapes. Whether you're seeking a peaceful retreat or an authentic cultural experience, our guest houses offer cozy rooms, local cuisine, and a chance to connect with the local community.
