About Saint Touma Monastery

Saint Touma Monastery, which is one of the few important religious and historical places in Lebanon named after Saint Thomas. The monastery dates back centuries, and is an attractive point for visitors who come to enjoy the tranquility, the natural scenery, and the spiritual atmosphere that distinguishes the town and the monastery. Mar Touma Monastery is distinguished by its ancient architectural style, which reflects a long history of worship and meditation. In addition to its religious importance, the monastery also provides wonderful views of the valley and surrounding mountains, and is a distinctive place for contemplation and tranquility away from the hustle and bustle of daily life.

It's History

The monastery was built in an old style. Years ago, the construction of this monastery was completed, because there was only the front section in the monastery (the altar and a little space after it, and the rest was destroyed), and it was restored and the other section was increased to become in the following form now. The monastery includes small shrines dedicated to Saint Thomas, in addition to some ancient icons.
