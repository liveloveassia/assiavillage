About Saydet l Najat

Our Lady of Perpetual Help (Saydet El Najat) is one of the titles given to the Virgin Mary, expressing believers’ deep faith in her role as an intercessor and helper in times of hardship and distress. This title has long symbolized Mary’s closeness to those who suffer and her willingness to offer comfort, strength, and divine assistance to all who seek her help with sincere hearts.

The name reflects people’s unwavering trust in the love and mercy of the Virgin Mary. Through prayer and devotion, believers turn to her to ask for spiritual guidance, divine aid, and inner peace. She is seen as a compassionate mother who never abandons her children in times of need.

This title also represents the tenderness and motherhood that Mary embodies toward her faithful followers. She is Our Lady of Help, overflowing with generosity and support for every person who calls upon her intercession with faith and humility.
